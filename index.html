Why Lekhapalani

Built for how Nepal actually spends

Most global trackers never supported Nepali wallets, cash or offline life. Lekhapalani starts where your money starts.

  • Quick add in 30 seconds

    Log any expense or income in under 30 seconds — cash, eSewa, Khalti or bank card. Pick a wallet, pick a category, done.

  • NPR-native wallets

    Track eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, FonePay, bank accounts and cash side by side — in Nepali rupees, with lakh and hajar formatting.

  • Envelope-style budgets

    Set monthly limits per category like a real khata. When a kharcha is running tight, Lekhapalani warns you before you overspend.

  • Reports that make sense

    Spend by category, month-over-month trends and savings streaks — the numbers that matter, in plain language, not spreadsheets.

  • Private by default

    Your financial data stays yours — on your device and in your exports. No ads, no selling your spending history.

  • Works offline, everywhere

    No internet? No problem. Manual entry works on a bus to Pokhara, a shop floor in Birgunj or a quiet evening at home.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Why do I need Lekhapalani when my wallet app shows statements?

Wallet statements show one account. Lekhapalani puts cash, eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay and your bank in one place, adds budgets and reports, and works even when you pay in cash — which most of Nepal still does.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Entry is fully offline and syncs when you're back online. This was a core requirement from day one — internet is not guaranteed in every shop, bus or village.

Is my financial data safe?

Your data lives on your device and in your exports. We don't run ads and we never sell spending history. [CLIENT-COPY: add privacy policy link and data-centre details]

Can I use it for my shop's khata too?

Yes — the Business Khata plan adds simple invoicing, a shopkeeper ledger and VAT-ready reports. Many members run personal and business books side by side.

How is this different from Khatapana?

Khatapana is a strong business-accounting app. Lekhapalani is built personal-first — household budgets, savings goals and reports for your own money — with a business tier on top. [CLIENT-COPY: refine positioning if needed]
