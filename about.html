Our story

A gap the big apps ignored

People kept asking the same question: which tracker works with eSewa and cash? The honest answer for years was none.

  • Mint and its peers never worked with Nepali banks, wallets or manual NPR entry
  • Households were tracking money in notebooks, spreadsheets and memory
  • Lekhapalani (लेखापालनि) is a centuries-old bookkeeping habit — we digitised it
