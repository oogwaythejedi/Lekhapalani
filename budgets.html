How budgeting works

Four steps to a month that balances

The envelope method is how Nepali households have budgeted for generations. We just removed the paper.

  1. 1

    Pick your categories

    Kirana, rent, school fees, transport, dining out, savings — start from a template or build your own.

  2. 2

    Set monthly limits

    Assign each envelope a limit in NPR based on last month's report and this month's plan.

  3. 3

    Log spending as usual

    Keep using quick add — every transaction is matched to its envelope automatically.

  4. 4

    Roll over or reset

    Unspent money rolls into next month. Overspend shows red, gently, before it's too late.

Budget features

Budgets that behave like a real khata

Simple rules, honest numbers, no guilt-tripping.

  • Envelope budgets

    Category limits with a live remaining balance for every envelope.

  • Shared household wallets

    One budget, several members — parents and partners see the same envelopes.

  • Rollover

    Surplus carries into next month automatically; overspend draws from the next envelope.

  • Overspend alerts

    A nudge when an envelope crosses 80%, and a clear warning at 100%.
