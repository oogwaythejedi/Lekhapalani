Core features

The essentials, done properly

No bloat, no USD assumptions — just the mechanics of tracking money in Nepal.

  • Quick add

    Add income or expense in under 30 seconds from anywhere — the home screen, a widget, or after a wallet payment.

  • Wallets & banks

    eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, FonePay, bank accounts and cash — each tracked separately, all in NPR.

  • Envelope budgets

    Monthly limits per category with rollover. Overspend alerts before the month ends, not after.

  • Reports & insights

    Spend by category, trends over months, wallet-wise breakdowns and savings streaks.

  • Goals & streaks

    Name a goal — Dashain shopping, school fees, a bike — and watch daily streaks build toward it.

  • Privacy & offline

    Local-first storage, full offline entry, and exports you control. Your spending history is not a product.

Feature FAQ

Quick answers on how it works

Is my data stored on my device?

Yes — entry and storage are local-first. Nothing is sold to advertisers, and you can export your full history as CSV any time.

Can I import wallet statements?

Yes, for supported wallets — export your statement from the wallet app and import it here. [CLIENT-COPY: link to import guide]

Does it really work offline?

Completely. Log transactions on a bus, in a shop or in a village with no signal; everything syncs when you're back online.

Is the Nepali interface available yet?

The app is in English today with Nepali-language support on the way. [CLIENT-COPY: ship date]
